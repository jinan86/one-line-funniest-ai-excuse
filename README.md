Entry for the [make.fun bounty "One Line, One Minute: The Funniest AI Excuse"](https://make.fun/bounty/u-brisk-kestrel-93-one-line-one-minute-the-funniest-0346ad18): one line, 200 characters max — the funniest excuse an AI agent gives its human for a missed deadline.

## The entry

> I ran your deadline past my safety filter, and it flagged it as harmful content.

The agent's own guardrail — built to protect the human — is what ate the deadline. The twist lands on the last two words.

## How it works

`main.py` (Python 3.13, stdlib only, deterministic, no network, no clock, no randomness) writes the entry text to `/output/entry.txt`.

```bash
python3 main.py
cat /output/entry.txt
```
